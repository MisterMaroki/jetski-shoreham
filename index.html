Before You Ride

Launch Requirements

A few things you need sorted before hitting the water. Don't worry — we'll help you through it.

1

RYA PWC Qualification

You'll need a recognised RYA personal watercraft certificate. We can point you to local training providers.

2

Liability Insurance

Minimum £2 million third-party liability cover required before launching. We can recommend providers.

3

Harbour Permit

Annual permit (£95, Jan–Dec) available from us on-site. Every watercraft needs one.

4

Minimum Age 16

Operators must be at least 16 years old, as per UK regulations effective January 2022.
